Support & Frequently Asked Questions

LanLink home

What settings do I need?

  • Name server: 203.0.113.1 (DSL) - your router learns it by itself
  • Mail (POP3): mail.lanlink.net, port 110
  • Outgoing mail (SMTP): mail.lanlink.net, port 25 (from our DSL lines only)
  • Your mail address: member name@lanlink.net, with your member password
  • News: news.lanlink.net

My DSL light is blinking

The modem is looking for the line. Check that the cable from the wall goes into the port marked LINE, not PHONE. Long or damaged cables between your router and computer can also make the connection slow; try a short one.

How do I put up a homepage?

Connect with any FTP program to members.lanlink.net with your user name and password, make a folder called public_html, and put an index.html in it. Under Linux:

  $ ftp members.lanlink.net
  ftp> mkdir public_html
  ftp> cd public_html
  ftp> put index.html

Is telnet still allowed?

Shell accounts on our servers were closed in 2002. Telnet sends your password in plain text across the net; if you run your own server, use SSH instead.
