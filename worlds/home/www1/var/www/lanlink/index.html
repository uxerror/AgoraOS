Welcome to LanLink!

LanLink connects homes and small businesses across the valley to the Internet. Dial-up, ISDN and now DSL up to 768k - with local people answering the phone when something goes wrong.

News

October 16, 2003 - DSL reaches the north side. The new equipment in the north exchange is up. If you live north of the river, call us to switch from dial-up. Your old e-mail address stays the same.

October 9, 2003 - Watch your attachments. A mass-mailing worm is going around with subjects like "Re: your document" and "Approved". Do not open attachments you did not ask for, even from people you know. Our mail servers now strip .pif and .scr files.

October 2, 2003 - Maintenance window. On Saturday October 25 between 2:00 and 4:00 AM the news server and member homepages will be down for a disk upgrade.

Homepages for everyone
