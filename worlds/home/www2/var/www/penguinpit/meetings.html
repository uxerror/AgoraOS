Meetings

We meet on the first Saturday of the month at the Valley Public Library. Everybody is welcome, from "what is a terminal" to "I wrote a driver".

October 4, 2003: Firewalls with iptables

Tom walked through a small firewall for a home server: drop what comes in from the outside unless it answers something you sent, allow SSH only from the LAN, and log the rest. His slides are in the HOWTO archive. The question everyone had: "so my router already does NAT, do I still need this?" Answer: yes, if you forward any ports to your box.

Afterwards Mike tried the 2.6.0-test6 kernel on his laptop. The ALSA sound driver did not load, then did, then the laptop would not suspend. Progress!
