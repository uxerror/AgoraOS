Building your own kernel (2.4.x)
================================  PenguinPit HOWTO, 2003

  cd /usr/src
  tar xjf linux-2.4.22.tar.bz2
  cd linux-2.4.22
  make menuconfig        (choose your hardware; say M for modules)
  make dep bzImage modules
  make modules_install
  cp arch/i386/boot/bzImage /boot/vmlinuz-2.4.22

Then add it to /etc/lilo.conf and run lilo. KEEP your old kernel in
lilo.conf, so you can boot it when the new one doesn't find your disk.
Everybody forgets IDE support once. Everybody.
