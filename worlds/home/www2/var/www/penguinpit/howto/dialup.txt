Dial-up with PPP on Debian
==========================  PenguinPit HOWTO, 2003

1. Find your modem. Internal "winmodems" mostly don't work under Linux;
   an external serial modem on /dev/ttyS0 always does.

2. Run pppconfig as root. It asks for:
     - a name for the connection (lanlink)
     - the phone number (see your provider's web site)
     - your user name and password (PAP)
     - the port (/dev/ttyS0)

3. Connect with "pon lanlink", hang up with "poff".
   Watch /var/log/messages to see what the modem says.

4. The modem screams while connecting because the two modems try
   speeds and agree on one. Turn the volume down with ATM0 in the
   init string if your family complains.
