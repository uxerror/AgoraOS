Ferret Crawls Past a Million Pages

Posted by drift on Saturday October 18, @04:40PM

Ferret started as a weekend project of two students and now indexes more than a million pages. Its crawler identifies itself as "Ferret" and obeys robots.txt, so webmasters can keep it out of parts of their sites. You can also submit your own pages with its Add URL form.

Robots.txt is not security (Score:5, Insightful)
by drift
Remember that robots.txt is a public file that lists exactly the directories you don't want people to look at. Well-behaved spiders stay out. Curious people read it first.
