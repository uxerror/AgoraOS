Fermion

Kaleidoscope - 64k intro - 1st place, PC intro competition, Byte Assembly 2003

Particles that fold into a city, a city that folds into a clock, and a soundtrack generated by a synthesizer written in 6 kilobytes. Needs a GeForce2 or better.

Download: ftp://ftp.demozone.net/pub/demos/fermion.zip (see the .nfo file next to it).
