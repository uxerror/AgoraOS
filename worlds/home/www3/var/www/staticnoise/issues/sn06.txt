  [0] on curiosity

  The best people we know are the ones who ask "how does this work?" and
  then take it apart to find out -- and put it back together, better.
  Curiosity is not a crime. Taking things that aren't yours is. Know the
  difference, and you'll never have to explain it to anyone in a suit.

  [1] a tour of the demoscene

  A demo is a program that makes music and pictures in real time, made to
  show off what a machine can do. Groups compete at parties; a 64k intro
  fits a whole show into a file smaller than this magazine. Start at
  www.demozone.net and watch the winners of this year's parties.
