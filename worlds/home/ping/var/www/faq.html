I forgot my Ping! number.

It is in the title of the messenger window, and in the file ~/.pingmsg/account in your home directory.

The messenger says "Connection refused".

Our server may be down for maintenance. If your friends can connect, check that your firewall lets you out to port 5190.

Can someone read my messages?

Messages travel as plain text, like e-mail. Anyone who can watch the network between you and us can read them. Don't send passwords or credit card numbers over Ping!.

Why do some friends show as offline when they are not?

They may be Invisible: they see you, you don't see them.

Someone I don't know sent me a message.

They show up under Not in list. You don't have to answer. Right-click them to see their info.
