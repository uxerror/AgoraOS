Talk to anyone, right now

Ping! Messenger shows you which of your friends are online and lets you send them messages that arrive in a second. No more waiting for e-mail.

Everyone on Ping! has a Ping! number. Get yours for free the first time you start the messenger, give it to your friends, and add theirs to your list.

Getting started

  1. Start Ping! Messenger from the menu of your desktop.
  2. Pick a password and press Get a new Ping! number.
  3. Press Add and type a friend's number, or Find to look people up by nick.
  4. Double-click a name to talk.

Away from the keyboard? Set your status to Away or N/A. Messages sent while you are offline wait on our server until you come back.

Our server

Ping! clients connect to login.pingmsg.com, TCP port 5190.
